## My Response
Correlation is a measure of how much one event tracks, or follows, another. Causation is proof that one event *causes* another. They can be the same but are not always the same. For instance, ice-cream sales and sunburn are strongly *correlated* because they both tend to increase at the same time. However, ice-cream sales do no *cause* sunburn; both are *caused* by hot, sunny weather.
